# Words every Modelica library needs and no English dictionary carries: the language's own
# vocabulary, the tools around it, and the engineering and mathematical terms the bundled
# en_US/en_GB dictionaries do not know.
#
# Always loaded, whichever dictionary languages a repository has chosen, and applied exactly like a
# repository's own accepted spellings (case-insensitive, and the possessive of an entry is accepted
# too). Lines starting with # are comments; there are no affix rules, so a form the dictionaries
# cannot derive - a plural, a participle - needs its own line.
#
# Entries here are dialect-neutral. A term whose American and British spellings differ, and that
# neither dictionary carries, lives in modelica_terms_en_US.txt / modelica_terms_en_GB.txt instead,
# so a repository is not quietly given the other dialect's spelling.
#
# Nothing here should be a word the bundled dictionaries already accept; that only makes the list
# look bigger than the gap it fills.
